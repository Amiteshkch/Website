About

I am a PhD graduate from IISc Bangalore working on atomization, droplet evaporation, and multiphase flow systems. My work combines high-speed experimental diagnostics with physics-based modeling.

Research Highlights

Rheology + imaging + droplet statistics used to link fluid properties with atomization efficiency.

Droplet Evaporation

Coupled heat & mass transfer modeling.

Fuel Spray in Crossflow

Gas turbine spray dynamics using laser diagnostics.
