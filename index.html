About

I am a PhD graduate from IISc Bangalore working at the intersection of fluid dynamics, heat & mass transfer, and experimental diagnostics. My work focuses on spray atomization, droplet evaporation, and energy systems relevant to gas turbines and spray drying.

Research & Projects

Rotary Atomization of Non-Newtonian Liquids

Studied viscoelastic polymer sprays using high-speed imaging and rheology.

Droplet Evaporation Modeling

Developed 1D transient evaporation model with Stefan flow and heat transfer coupling.

Fuel Spray in Crossflow

Experimental investigation of atomization and evaporation in gas turbine combustors.
