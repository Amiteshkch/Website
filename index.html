About Me

I am a PhD graduate from IISc Bangalore working on experimental and numerical investigation of atomization and droplet evaporation processes. My work bridges fundamental fluid mechanics with real-world applications in gas turbine combustion and spray drying systems.

Research Highlights

Rotary Atomization of Complex Fluids

Investigated breakup dynamics of viscoelastic liquids under high-speed rotary atomization. Combined rheology, imaging, and droplet analysis.

Droplet Evaporation

Developed experimental setup and 1D transient model incorporating heat and mass transfer coupling and Stefan flow effects.
